# StarCraft TMG: Mission Cards

Source: Scenario Cards - Mission & Deployment pages on the Terran, Zerg, and Protoss P2P Card Sheets (A4), plus the Protoss P2P W2 Card Sheet for Artefact Hunt. Card text is transcribed from the card sheets.

Mission cards other than Artefact Hunt and Fire in the Sky print the same text on the Terran, Zerg, and Protoss sheets. Artefact Hunt appears on the Protoss sheet only. Fire in the Sky appears on the Terran W3 sheet only. Deployment cards are graphical and are not transcribed here.

## Supply Drop

**Standard Engagement** (Supply 6, Per Round +2, Game Length 5)

- **Mission Parameters:** All Mission Markers are Deactivated. START OF THE ROUND (1-4): Randomly determine one Deactivated Mission Marker (1-4) and flip it to its Activated side. START OF THE ROUND 5: Mission Marker 5 is automatically Activated.
- **Scoring Conditions:** Score VPs equal to Enemy Supply destroyed this Round. FROM THE START OF THE FIRST ROUND: For each activated Controlled Mission Marker, gain VPs equal to the Game Round number in which that Mission Marker was Activated and remove it.
- **Additional Conditions:** SPECIAL WINNING CONDITIONS: The game ends immediately if a Player leads by 12+ VPs.

**Skirmish Level** (Supply 4, Per Round +1, Game Length 4)

- **Mission Parameters:** All Mission Markers are Deactivated. START OF THE ROUND (2-4): Randomly determine one Deactivated Mission Marker (1,2,5) and flip it to its Activated side.
- **Scoring Conditions:** Score VPs equal to Enemy Supply destroyed this Round. FROM THE START OF THE SECOND ROUND: For each activated Controlled Mission Marker, gain VPs equal to the Game Round number in which that Mission Marker was Activated and remove it.
- **Additional Conditions:** SPECIAL WINNING CONDITIONS: The game ends immediately if a Player leads by 8+ VPs.

## Hold Position

**Standard Engagement** (Supply 6, Per Round +2, Game Length 5)

- **Mission Parameters:** All Mission Markers are Activated.
- **Scoring Conditions:** Score VPs equal to Enemy Supply destroyed this Round. FROM THE START OF THE SECOND ROUND: Gain 1 VP for each Controlled Mission Marker that is Neutral or associated with your colour. Gain 2 VPs for each Controlled Mission Marker associated with the Opponent colour.
- **Additional Conditions:** SPECIAL WINNING CONDITIONS: The game ends immediately if a Player leads by 10+ VPs.

## Gather the Resources

**Standard Engagement** (Supply 6, Per Round +2, Game Length 5)

- **Mission Parameters:** All Mission Markers are Activated.
- **Scoring Conditions:** Score VPs equal to Enemy Supply destroyed this Round. FROM THE START OF THE SECOND ROUND: Gain 2 VPs for each Controlled Mission Marker associated with the Opponent colour.
- **Additional Conditions:** ASSAULT PHASE: If an Unengaged Unit is Within 3" of a Controlled Neutral Mission Marker or associated with the Opponent, this unit may perform the Gather Action instead of a standard action. GATHER ACTION: Gain 1 VP. SPECIAL WINNING CONDITIONS: The game ends immediately if a Player leads by 10+ VPs.

## Divide and Conquer

**Standard Engagement** (Supply 8, Per Round +2, Game Length 4)

- **Mission Parameters:** All Mission Markers are Activated. Split the Battlefield in four quarters as shown on the Deployment Card.
- **Scoring Conditions:** Score VPs equal to Enemy Supply destroyed this Round. FROM THE START OF THE FIRST ROUND: Calculate Total Current Supply in each battlefield quarter. Gain 1 VP for each quarter where your Total Current Supply is higher than the Opponent's. Only Units Wholly Within a quarter contribute their Supply to this check. If a Unit controls a Mission Marker, that Unit is worth 1 extra Supply for controlling quarters. Gain 2 VP for Controlling Mission Marker 5.
- **Additional Conditions:** SPECIAL WINNING CONDITIONS: The game ends immediately if a Player leads by 10+ VPs.

**Skirmish Level** (Supply 4, Per Round +1, Game Length 4)

- **Mission Parameters:** All Mission Markers are Activated. Split the Battlefield in four quarters as shown on the Deployment Card.

## Artefact Hunt

Protoss P2P W2 Card Sheet. Transcribed from a lower-resolution render than the other missions; verify the figures against the card before relying on them.

**Standard Engagement** (Supply 6, Per Round +2, Game Length 5)

- **Mission Parameters:** All Mission Markers are Activated.
- **Scoring Conditions:** Score VPs equal to Enemy Supply destroyed this Round. END OF ROUND 5: Gain 4 VP for each Friendly Unit that CLAIM Mission Marker and it is Wholly Within Friendly Zone of Influence. Gain 2 VP for each Friendly Mission Marker associated with your colour that is not currently CLAIM by an Enemy Unit.
- **Additional Conditions:** SCORING PHASE: From the Start of the Second Round, if a Unit is Within 3" of a Controlled Mission Marker that is Neutral or associated with the Opponent colour, that Unit may CLAIM it. CLAIM: Place the Mission Marker on the Unit Card. A Unit carrying a Mission Marker must DROP it if: It has its Current Supply Value reduced. Before it resolves a PLACE effect. It is Engaged with Enemies having a greater combined Current Supply Value. It is Destroyed, removed from the battlefield, or returned to Reserves. When a Unit DROPs a Mission Marker, the Opponent places the Mission Marker Within 2" of the Unit or its last model removed. Unit may CLAIM only one Mission Marker at a time.

**Skirmish Level** (Supply 3, Per Round +1, Game Length 5)

- **Mission Parameters:** All Mission Markers are Activated.
- **Scoring Conditions:** Score VPs equal to Enemy Supply destroyed this Round. END OF ROUND 5: Gain 3 VP for each Friendly Unit that CLAIM Mission Marker and it is Wholly Within Friendly Zone of Influence. Gain 1 VP for each Friendly Mission Marker associated with your colour that is not currently CLAIM by an Enemy Unit.
- **Additional Conditions:** SCORING PHASE: From the Start of the Second Round, if a Unit is Within 3" of a Controlled Mission Marker that is Neutral or associated with the Opponent colour, that Unit may CLAIM it. CLAIM: Place the Mission Marker on the Unit Card. A Unit carrying a Mission Marker must DROP it if: It has its Current Supply Value reduced. Before it resolves a PLACE effect. It is Engaged with Enemies having a greater combined Current Supply Value. It is Destroyed, removed from the battlefield, or returned to Reserves. When a Unit DROPs a Mission Marker, the Opponent places the Mission Marker Within 2" of the Unit or its last model removed. Unit may CLAIM only one Mission Marker at a time.

## Fire in the Sky

Terran P2P W3 Card Sheet.

**Standard Engagement** (Supply 5, Per Round +3, Game Length 5)

- **Mission Parameters:** All Mission Markers are Activated. During battlefield setup, place a D6 next to each Mission Marker to track its Sabotage Level. Set the starting Sabotage Level of each Mission Marker to 1.
- **Scoring Conditions:** Score VPs equal to Enemy Supply destroyed this Round. FROM THE START OF THE SECOND ROUND: Gain VP for each Controlled Mission Marker that is Neutral or associated with the Opponent colour. The VP gained is equal to the Sabotage Level on the marker. Sabotage Level 3 = 3 VP. Sabotage Level 2 = 2 VP. Sabotage Level 1 = 1 VP.
- **Additional Conditions:** ASSAULT PHASE: If an Unengaged Unit is Within 3" of a Controlled Mission Marker, this unit may perform Sabotage Action instead of a standard action. Sabotage Action: Decrease or Increase that Mission Marker's Sabotage Level by 1 (to a minimum of 1 or maximum of 3). SPECIAL WINNING CONDITIONS: The game ends immediately if a Player leads by 10+ VPs.

**Skirmish Level** (Supply 3, Per Round +2, Game Length 5)

- **Mission Parameters:** All Mission Markers are Activated. During battlefield setup, place a D6 next to each Mission Marker to track its Sabotage Level. Set the starting Sabotage Level of each Mission Marker to 1.
- **Scoring Conditions:** Score VPs equal to Enemy Supply destroyed this Round. FROM THE START OF THE SECOND ROUND: Gain VP for each Controlled Mission Marker that is Neutral or associated with the Opponent colour. The VP gained is equal to the Sabotage Level on the marker. Sabotage Level 3 = 3 VP. Sabotage Level 2 = 2 VP. Sabotage Level 1 = 1 VP.
- **Additional Conditions:** ASSAULT PHASE: If an Unengaged Unit is Within 3" of a Controlled Mission Marker, this unit may perform Sabotage Action instead of a standard action. Sabotage Action: Decrease or Increase that Mission Marker's Sabotage Level by 1 (to a minimum of 1 or maximum of 3). SPECIAL WINNING CONDITIONS: The game ends immediately if a Player leads by 8+ VPs.
