# StarCraft: Tabletop Miniature Game FAQ

FAQ V1.1, dated 25 September 2026.

Source: "StarCraft TMG FAQ", FAQ V1.1. Transcribed word for word. The table of contents and the closing thank-you note are not reproduced.

---

## Units & Characteristics

**Q: With the Move or Run actions, can I move 0”?**
A: No. A Unit must actually change its position on the battlefield when performing a Move or Run action. If a Unit does not reposition, it is considered to have performed a Hold action instead.

**Q: How do Shields work, and can they be healed?**
A: A Unit’s Shield value is added directly to the Hit Points (HP) of its first model. For example, a Zealot (4 HP) with a Shield of 3 gives its first model a total of 7 HP. Lost HP from this combined pool can be healed normally.

**Q: When exactly is SHIELDED Status lost?**
A: SHIELDED Status is lost in either of the following two situations:
- The Total Damage assigned to the Unit exceeds its Shield value. For example, a Unit with a Shield of 3 only loses its status after taking 4 or more damage, not 3. Because this is checked after damage is resolved, any abilities that reduce incoming damage before it is assigned can prevent the loss of SHIELDED Status.
- The first model in the Unit is removed.

Once SHIELDED Status is lost, it cannot be restored by healing.

**Q: How does non-lethal damage interact with SHIELDED Status?**
A: Non-lethal damage is added to the Unit’s Total Damage Pool and removes SHIELDED Status exactly like standard damage.

**Q: Can a destroyed Unit return to the game?**
A: No. Once its last model is removed and a Unit is Destroyed, it cannot return to play. Unless specified otherwise in mission or other rules.

## Measuring & Movement

**Q: How do you check if a Unit is Out of Coherency for the purpose of contesting objectives?**
A: Unit Coherency is checked only at the end of a movement. If the Unit passed its last check, it remains in Coherency for objective control, regardless of any casualties suffered since then.

**Q: Does the Leading Model have to move its maximum distance during a “directly towards” or “directly away” move?**
A: Yes. The Leading Model must move the maximum possible distance Directly Towards or Directly Away from the target, as applicable.

**Q: What happens when a model cannot fit its base on High Ground?**
A: Apply the Wobbly Model Syndrome rules. The centre of the model’s base must remain on the High Ground terrain feature, and the base may overhang the edge by the minimum amount necessary to stand.

**Q: How do Gap Clearance checks work with High Ground, and can you move past a model that is less than an inch from its edge?**
A: The edge of a High Ground feature is treated as Impassable Terrain for Gap Clearance purposes. Consequently, any physical space must satisfy the moving model’s Size requirement (1” wide for Size 2 or lower, and 3” wide for Size 3 or larger Units). For example, a Size 3 Siege Tank can drive up a ramp if the gap between the ramp’s edges is at least 3” wide, and it can be placed at the top using the Wobbly Model Syndrome rules if its base cannot fit entirely.

**Q: Can a Ground Unit move directly between unconnected High Grounds?**
A: No. Ground Units can only move between connected High Grounds.

## The Battlefield

**Q: Can a model gain Direct Cover by being within 1” of any part of a terrain piece, even if that specific part is not between the model and the attacker, so long as the Line of Sight trace passes through that terrain?**
A: Yes. Direct Cover is established as long as the Line of Sight trace passes through a terrain piece and the model is Within 1” of any part of that same terrain piece. It does not matter if the specific section of the terrain Within 1” is the part physically obstructing the trace.

**Q: Do Gap Clearance rules apply to gaps between models, or only to gaps in or between terrain?**
A: They apply to both. A gap is defined as any physical space between terrain pieces or models through which a moving model’s base must pass.

**Q: Can models be placed on the other side of an enemy unit during a Charge or Close Ranks action, even if there are no gaps?**
A: Yes. Coherency Links may pass through Enemy models that your Unit is currently Engaged with. As long as there is open space to place the bases within Coherency, models can be set on the other side of the enemy.

**Q: Can an objective marker be controlled while it is Deactivated?**
A: Yes. Deactivated Mission Markers can still be controlled normally. The rules for determining control apply to all Mission Markers currently on the battlefield, regardless of whether they are flipped to their Activated or Deactivated side.

**Q: Can a Coherency Link pass through terrain, models, and gaps that the Leading Model could move through, or only gaps?**
A: It can pass through anything the Leading Model can legally traverse. For example, since Zerg Raptors have special movement rules allowing them to traverse IMPASSABLE TERRAIN, other models in the Unit can trace their Coherency Links directly through that terrain piece to stay connected.

**Q: During the Mission and Deployment draft, when both players have the same card, do I remove a specific card or the card name?**
A: You block only the specific physical card, not its name. Since players can bring identical cards, removing one physical copy from the table does not discard the duplicate copy, which remains a valid choice.

**Q: Are the long walls shown in the official map layouts treated as a single terrain feature or as multiple independent terrain pieces?**
A: They are treated as multiple independent terrain pieces.

**Q: Can I place a model onto High Ground from an Access Point using Coherency, or do I need to perform a Move action?**
A: You can do so via Coherency, no separate Move action is required. When a Unit resolves a movement, only the nominated Leading Model measures and moves along a physical path. All other models in the Unit are set on the battlefield, and Coherency Links are explicitly allowed to trace through Access Points to connect models across different elevation levels.

**Q: Can a Flying or BURROWED Unit claim a Controlled Mission Marker in “Artefact Hunt”?**
A: Yes. Rules blocking them from Controlling or Contesting Mission Markers do not prevent them from claiming said markers in the “Artefact Hunt”.

**Q: In Supply Drop, which occurs first: rolling for Mission Marker Activation or the Priority Roll?**
A: Rolling for Mission Marker Activation. The roll for Mission Marker Activation is resolved before players roll for Priority.

**Q: Can Units benefit from the Creep speed buff during their Deploy move?**
A: No. Units in Reserves cannot use abilities or measure range until they are placed on the battlefield. Because a Unit’s passive abilities do not function while in Reserves and the Unit cannot be “On Creep” before actually entering the board, the Deploy must be resolved using the Unit’s standard Speed characteristic.

## Deployment, Entry Edges, & Zone of Influence

**Q: If High Ground is touching the edge of the board, can a Unit deploy directly onto it?**
A: Yes. Models may deploy directly onto High Ground if the player’s Entry Edge physically touches that terrain feature. All standard deployment requirements still apply, meaning the Unit must be placed legally in Coherency.

## Attack Sequence

**Q: Do Evade rolls work against Surge?**
A: Yes. Evade rolls are resolved against the final Damage Pool, which includes Surge. Surge only bypasses the Armour Roll, moving successful hits directly to the Damage Pool.

**Q: If High Ground is touching the edge of an Omega Worm or Pylon base, can a Unit deploy directly onto it?**
A: No. Units may not deploy onto a different elevation level than the Unit they are deploying from.

**Q: Can ANTI-EVADE (or any other modifier) ever negate an Evade roll (or any other check) entirely?**
A: No. Target numbers can never be modified below 2+ or above 6+. Because abilities like ANTI-EVADE apply modifiers, they can only worsen a check to a maximum of 6+.

**Q: Does the Omega Network’s ability count as a player’s Entry Edge for rules like Photon Overcharge?**
A: Yes. Both the Omega Worm and a Pylon’s base are treated as friendly Entry Edges owned by the player who placed them.
- Because they are Friendly Entry Edges, you can trigger abilities like Burrow Ambush from them, and they are immune to enemy denial effects like Photon Overcharge.
- Standard forward-deployment cards and transports (e.g., Proxy Barrack, or Warp Prism) do not create Entry Edges. They simply grant a temporary exception to the rules to deploy without one.,

**Q: Can Ranged Attacks destroy target models that are outside of weapon range if they are within Line of Sight?**
A: Yes. Casualty removal is restricted only by visibility, not range. Any model in the target Unit that is visible to at least one model in the attacking Unit is eligible to be removed as a casualty, even if that specific model is physically outside of the weapon’s range.

**Q: Can a Unit deployed through a Pylon as an Entry Edge perform any additional actions or resolve abilities?**
A: No. Deploying a Unit this way consumes its entire Activation, which ends immediately upon setup. Once the Unit is placed on the battlefield, you must place an Activation Marker next to it, preventing it from performing any further actions or resolving any other active abilities during that Phase.

**Q: When a Unit is engaged, and all of its models are in base-to-base contact, can you perform a Close Ranks action?**
A: No. Since the action requires the Leading Model to end their movement closer to the enemy than they started, a Unit where everyone is already in base-to-base contact cannot resolve a Close Ranks action.

**Q: How are models removed in close combat when fighting multiple enemy Units at once?**
A: You must prioritise maintaining engagement with ALL enemy Units. When removing casualties at Priority 2 or 3, you cannot choose a model if its removal would break engagement with a specific enemy Unit, as long as another valid casualty exists in any priority tier.

**Q: Does the Omega Network’s ability generate a Zone of Influence (the restricted area extending 6” from a player’s Entry Edge)?**
A: No. Zones of Influence are only generated by the primary Entry Edges defined by your Deployment Card and are marked exclusively during battlefield setup.

**Q: How does deployment work for end-of-round deployment abilities (like Ventral Sacs, Ready For Dust-off, or Warp Conduit) that place a Unit from a marker?**
A: Place the leading model in base-to-base contact with the marker, and the remaining models in Unit Coherency completely outside of enemy Engagement Range. If you cannot place the leading model in base-to-base contact with the marker while keeping it outside of enemy Engagement Range, the Unit cannot be deployed, and the ability is wasted.

**Q: When a Unit has multiple different ranged weapons, must you declare all targets before you begin rolling to hit?**
A: No. Targets are declared and resolved one weapon Batch at a time. You select a target for your first weapon type, resolve those attacks completely, and only then declare the target for your next weapon type.

## Abilities & Tactics Cards

**Q: In what order are attacks using a Blast Template resolved?**
A: Attacks using a Blast Template may be resolved in any order, keeping in mind that Spillover attacks cannot receive any modifiers.

**Q: Can a Special Ability be used before deploying a Unit (e.g., triggering Stimpack to increase its Speed)?**
A: No. Abilities do not function while a Unit is in Reserves. Only abilities that explicitly state they work in Reserves (such as “Raiders Roll!”) or those that actually deploy the Unit (like “Armed and Ready” or “Phase Prism”) can be resolved before deployment.

**Q: How do CP, BM, and PE resources work when paying for special abilities?**
A: You must exhaust (flip face-down) one or more Ready cards matching the required resource type to pay the cost. Any excess resources generated are lost and cannot be saved or spent on another ability. For example, exhausting a card that produces 2 CP to pay for a 1 CP ability immediately discards the leftover 1 CP.

**Q: What does “Nominated to deploy” mean?**
A: This term refers to a Unit actively performing a Deploy Action. Any card effect, special ability, or core game rule that causes a Unit to perform a Deploy Action counts as that Unit being nominated to deploy.

**Q: Is a Unit considered within range of itself for its own abilities?**
A: Yes. A Unit is always considered within range of itself unless a rule explicitly states otherwise.

**Q: Does activating a Tactical Card to use one of its abilities require an active Unit on the battlefield, even if the card doesn’t directly affect that Unit?**
A: Yes. An active Unit on the battlefield is always required to resolve a card’s Active Ability.

**Q: Can a Unit use any number of Active abilities or Tactical Cards during its Activation?**
A: Yes. A Unit can resolve any number of differently named Active abilities or Tactical Cards during its Activation, provided you can pay their resource costs. However, Reactions are restricted to one per Activation. Named Active abilities cannot be used more than Once per Round per Unit unless a rule explicitly states otherwise (e.g., a single Marine Unit cannot trigger the “Go! Go! Go!” ability twice in the same Round).

**Q: When resolving the Marauder’s Concussive Shells reaction, do you need Line of Sight to the charging Enemy Unit or to the Friendly Unit being charged?**
A: You only need Line of Sight to the Friendly Unit. Because the ability is measured and targeted toward a Friendly Unit Within 8”, that friendly unit is the subject of the range and visibility check.

**Q: Can you use the Advanced Training reaction from the Academy if the card is already exhausted?**
A: No. Once a Tactical Card is Exhausted, none of its printed abilities can be resolved or used until the card is Refreshed during the Phase 4 Cleanup.

**Q: Can you resolve Reaction Abilities outside of an activation?**
A: Yes. Reactions can be resolved at any time their specific trigger conditions are met, regardless of whether a Unit is currently activated.
- Outside of activations, you are restricted to resolving only one Reaction per trigger Phase (provided the participating Unit has not already used that reaction this round).
- If both players attempt to react to the same trigger outside of an activation, the holder of the First Player Marker resolves theirs first.

**Q: Can Raptors stack the IMPACT modifiers from Malignant Creep, the Evolution Chamber, and their Adrenal Overload ability?**
A: Yes. Because these abilities apply numeric modifiers rather than keywords, they stack. Modifiers from different named sources are cumulative, allowing these three effects to combine for a potential +3 bonus.

**Q: Can you stack Accelerating Creep with the Queen’s Creep Speed, Wild Mutation, or Roach Glial Reconstitution?**
A: Movement modifiers from different named sources stack normally, unless they share the BUFF keyword. Because Accelerating Creep and the Queen’s Creep Speed upgrade apply flat modifiers without using keywords, they stack with other speed bonuses. However, both Wild Mutation and Glial Reconstitution grant the BUFF Speed keyword, meaning they do not stack with each other.

**Q: What counts as targeting a Structure?**
A: Abilities can affect a Structure if they target an area. or the Unit itself rather than a specific individual model. Structures are only excluded from an ability’s effects if that ability’s targeting requirements, keywords, or restrictions rule them out.

**Q: What is the difference between “select” and “target”?**
A: There is no mechanical or rules distinction between these two terms. They are treated as synonyms.

**Q: Can an opponent place their models on a Faction Indicator (from Ventral Sacs, Ready For Dust-off, or Warp Conduit)?**
A: Yes. Opponents can freely move onto and block a Faction Indicator. If the indicator becomes blocked so that you can no longer place a deploying model in base-to-base contact with it, the indicator remains on the field until the End of the Round and is then removed without deploying the Unit.

**Q: Can an Adept Unit use Psionic Transfer to “teleport” while carrying a claimed Mission Marker in the “Artefact Hunt” mission?**
A: No.

**Q: Can Raynor’s Raiders use their Rapid Reinforcement ability to PLACE themselves from a Unit with the Structure Special Ability?**
A: No.

**Q: How do you resolve the PLACE (0) effect of Artanis’s Phase Prism ability?**
A: Place Artanis directly in base-to-base contact with a model in the selected target Unit.

**Q: Does the Combat Shield Special Ability grant an Evade roll against Non-Lethal Damage, Psionic Storm, or Plague?**
A: Yes.

**Q: Can damage already assigned to a Unit be removed?**
A: Yes. Damage reduction applies to a Unit’s Total Damage (the sum of existing and newly assigned damage). For example, if a Marauder Unit with 2 damage takes 1 additional damage, a Medic’s “Life Support” can remove all 3 damage.

**Q: Can Roaches move through a Force Field using Tunnelling Claws?**
A: No. Tunnelling Claws only allow a Unit to move through enemy Units, not Terrain. Since a Force Field is classified as Terrain, it cannot be bypassed or moved through using this ability.

**Q: Can a Size 3 Unit be PLACED into a Force Field to automatically remove it?**
A: No. Because a PLACE effect is not a movement, it cannot remove a Force Field.

**Q: Can Raptors move across Force Fields, since they can normally bypass terrain?**
A: No. Force Fields explicitly forbid Units of Size 2 or less from moving across them. Because Raptors are Size 1, they cannot ignore this specific terrain restriction.

**Q: When is ON CREEP checked for the movement bonus from Accelerating Creep?**
A: At the start of the movement. If a Unit is ON CREEP when it begins its movement, it gains the bonus for the entire move, even if it leaves Creep during that movement.

## Keywords

**Q: Does PINPOINT allow a Unit to ignore its own engagement and shoot at outside targets, or does it only allow targeting an engaged enemy?**
A: It only allows shooting into combat, not shooting out of it. The keyword overrides the targeting restriction on the target Unit, allowing models to shoot at Engaged enemies.

**Q: Does a MORPHED Unit award Victory Points (VPs) to the opponent when the Units’ Supply Value is reduced?**
A: Yes. A MORPHED Unit awards Victory Points (VPs) exactly like any other Unit on the battlefield, equal to the amount of Supply lost.

**Q: Can I remove a Creep Token by using the PLACE keyword?**
A: No. Creep Removal only triggers when an enemy Unit ends a Move, Deploy, Run, Charge, or Disengage action within 1” of a Creep Tumor. As PLACE is neither of those, ending a PLACE movement near a Creep Tumor does not remove it.

**Q: If a Specialist model (like an AGG-12 Marine) is removed, can you keep the weapon in play by replacing another model that doesn’t have it?**
A: No. You cannot transfer Specialist models to other models or replace a model without the weapon to keep the Specialist.

**Q: To benefit from the LOCKED IN keyword, must the attacking model be stationary, or does the target need to have Stationary Status?**
A: The target must have the Stationary Status.

**Q: Can I use the Psionic Transfer ability to SET Adepts within Engagement Range?**
A: Yes.

**Q: How does Indirect Fire work against a target Unit when only some of its models are visible?**
A: The target Unit does not gain an Evade roll, and non-visible models can still be removed as casualties. Because at least one model is visible, the target Unit itself is considered to be within Line of Sight, meaning it does not qualify for the Evade roll granted to out-of-sight targets.

## Templates & Spillover

**Q: How do Blast Template abilities (like Psionic Storm or Plague) resolve without a physical “Primary Target” to establish a Target Point, Elevation Level, or Combat Tags?**
A: You resolve them using the template rules with the following key adaptations:
- The Unit executing the ability is treated as the Attacking Model.
- The point directly beneath the centre of the template is treated as the Target Point and the Primary Target.
- Because this Target Point has no Combat Tags, spillover targets do not need to match any targeting tags to be affected.
- Affected models must still occupy the same Elevation Level as the Target Point. For these attacks, Flying Units are treated as occupying the same Elevation Level as their physical bases on the battlefield.

**Q: Since HIDDEN Units cannot be targeted from more than 4” away, how can “detection” abilities (such as Scanner Sweep, Oversight Mode, or Surveillance) affect them at a 6” range?**
A: These abilities target a location on the battlefield rather than the Units themselves. They place a Faction Indicator to create a 6” area of effect. Because the ability does not select the Unit as a target, it bypasses the 4” targeting restriction.

**Q: When are abilities or effects with a “while within” condition checked?**
A: These effects are checked at the start and the end of any action or ability during the game round. If a Unit is within the specified range and meets all other requirements, the effect applies immediately.

**Q: Does the Spillover requirement to “share the same Combat Tag as the Primary target” prevent a template from spilling over to models with different tags (e.g., from Light Zerglings to Armoured Roaches), or is it only meant to check Ground vs. Flying status?**
A: Spillover targets only need to share at least one Combat Tag and match the elevation level of the Primary target. Since Zerglings and Roaches both share the Ground Combat Tags, a template can spill over between them if they are on the same elevation. Mutalisks cannot be affected by Ground-level templates even though they share the Biological tag, because Flying Units do not occupy a standard Elevation Level for template purposes.

**Q: Can a Reaction ability with the REPEATABLE keyword be used multiple times during the same Activation?**
A: No. The standard limit of one Reaction per Activation still applies. While REPEATABLE allows the ability to be used across different Activations during the round, it cannot be used multiple times in the same Activation. For triggers that occur outside of an Activation entirely, the ability can only be used once per trigger.

**Q: Do keywords like PRECISION, SURGE, or CRITICAL HIT apply to individual models, or do they apply to the entire Unit?**
A: They apply to the weapon Batch (the collective dice pool generated by the Unit’s attack) rather than to individual models. Models in a Unit using the same weapon profile combine their Rate of Attack (RoA) dice into a single pool, and these keywords act on the dice within that pool. Individual models are tracked separately only to determine Range, visibility, and specific per-model roll modifiers (such as the -1 Hit penalty for Long Range).

**Q: How does Guardian Shield interact with Spillover?**
A: Because Spillover hits are resolved as separate attack batches, the Guardian Shield modifier applies to each batch individually. Any Ranged Attack (including both the main target and any Spillover batches) targeting a friendly Unit affected by the Guardian Shield is made with 1 fewer die in its Attack Pool.

**Q: How do abilities like PRECISION work when splitting attacks in combat against multiple enemy Units?**
A: Splitting attacks is resolved as a single Batch, allowing you to allocate keyword effects as they are resolved. Because the attacks against both Units occur simultaneously, you can choose to apply keywords like PRECISION entirely to one target’s pool or split them across both pools.

**Q: How do keywords such as PRECISION (X) or CRITICAL HIT (X) interact with Spillover?**
A: Weapon keywords apply exclusively to the Attack Pool generated against the Main Target. They do not carry over to Spillover batches, which are resolved as separate, basic attack batches without modifiers.

## A Special Thank You

**Q: Does morphing a Unit award Victory Points (VPs) to the opponent if the original Unit’s Supply Value is reduced?**
A: Yes. Anytime a Unit’s Current Supply Value is reduced, including during a Morph, the opponent gains Victory Points (VPs) equal to the amount of Supply lost.
