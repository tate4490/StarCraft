# PART 1: LEARN TO PLAY

Part of [SCTMG Core Rules](../SCTMG%20Core%20Rules.md). Source pages 5 to 28.

## Worked Example: Starting Rounds [p. 5]

The battles in StarCraft: Tabletop Miniature Game are fast-paced, similar to those played on-screen for many years. To help each player understand the game's flow, we've prepared a battle report that will serve as a great introduction for new players.

Below are the first two rounds of the game between the TERRANS, played by Katherine, and the ZERGS, played by James.

They both agree to choose a Mission and Deployment Card together to create a classic clash on no man's land. The objective of the Frontlines mission is to take control of up to 3 Mission Markers to gain Victory Points (VPs), gaining even more if any of them succeed in retaking objectives controlled by the Enemy. Abandoned Camp is a relatively standard deployment - each player claims one whole border of the playmat to be their respective Entry Edge.

After that, they prepare the battlefield together, placing terrain in a way that allows them to learn the game and make the most of their actions, special abilities, and tactic cards.

Once the battlefield is set up, the dice are prepared, and both players are ready to play, they roll for initiative in the first Round!

**TERRAN**

Units:

- 6 Marines - With two upgrades: 1x AGG-12, which replaces one C-14 rifle and 1x Rocket Launcher, with the SIDEARM keyword, which is added onto a model equipped with a C-14 rifle,
- 6 Marines,
- 2 Marauders,
- 3 Medics.

Tactical Card:

- Barracks.

Faction Card:

- Terran Armed Forces.

**ZERG**

Units:

- 12 Zerglings - With the Shredding Claws upgrade, which replaces their regular Claws - it's equipped on all models in the Unit,
- 12 Zerglings,
- 3 Roaches - With the Burrow Ambush upgrade,
- 1 Queen.

Tactical Cards:

- Roach Warren.

Faction Card:

- Zerg Swarm.

Special Card:

- Accelerating Creep.

## Round 1: Movement Phase [p. 6]

They both roll 2d6: James rolls 7, and Katherine rolls 5. James wins and decides that the Terrans under Katherine's command will start the first round.

As their first action, Katherine brings a Unit of Marines from Reserves, equipped with special weapons. She moves this unit towards the middle to cover the area with all of her weapons.

James responds with his basic Unit of Zerglings that have a passive Special Ability which allows him to put the models in this unit In Coherency 4", and he tries to hide from the Marines. He hopes that as many models as possible survive before he's in range for close combat.

[p. 7]

Katherine deploys a Unit of Medics that can provide healing. This Unit is very vulnerable, so she puts all models behind the Marines.

James uses the Burrow Ambush special ability, which allows the Unit to be set up within 18" of his Entry Edge, and puts the Roaches 2" from the Mission Marker.

[p. 8]

To counter these Roaches, Katherine deploys her heavy guns onto the field - Marauders. This unit has an anti-armour ranged weapon that can do considerable damage to Roaches. After deploying this Unit, she uses the "Go! Go! Go!" special ability from the Barracks tactical card.

[p. 9]

James wants to add some survivability to his Army, so he deploys a Queen.

After moving onto Battlefield, James uses Queen's Active Ability "Spawn Creep Tumor" and puts a Creep Tumor token in base contact. He needs to pay 1 Biomass for this ability, but he can reduce that cost by 1 with "Psionic Link" Passive Ability, because there are 7 Friendly models Within 6" of the Queen.

Additionally, to save the life of James' favourite Roaches, he uses the "Rapid Burrowing" special ability from the Zerg Swarm Faction Card. Now, the Roaches are BURROWED and HIDDEN. Katherine can't target them with her Marauders.

Katherine has deployed all units up to the limit of 3 Supply this Round, so she must Pass and claim the First Player Marker for the next Phase. James also reached that limit and must Pass as well.

## Round 1: Assault Phase [p. 10]

Katherine decides that the Zerg will activate first. She could go first, but the only juicy target - the Roaches - is HIDDEN and BURROWED. She wants to wait and force the Zergs to move.

James activates Queen and moves 5" towards the middle of the battlefield.

Katherine responds by moving Medics towards the middle. In a future activation, they will be able to heal the Marine unit.

James activates Zerglings, which move towards the middle, and Katherine responds with Marines, which also move towards the middle. In future turns, it will be very important who goes first.

[p. 11]

James activates Roaches, uses the "Nasty Surprise" special ability from the Roach Warren Tactical Card to remove the BURROWED Status, and declares a Charge action against the Marauders. After checking the range, Roaches need to move at least 7" to reach their target. As a reaction to the charge, Katherine uses the "Concussive Shells" Special Ability of the Marauders to reduce the charge distance of the Roaches by 2". She uses the Terran Armed Forces Tactical Card to pay the cost. Unfortunately, James rolls a 6 for charge distance, so it is enough to engage the Marauders in close combat.

After finishing the charge, James uses the IMPACT Special Ability of the Roaches. He rolls 6 dice and hits 3 of them. Marauders have an Armour characteristic of 4+ and need to roll 3 dice. Luckily, they succeeded in blocking two hits, receiving only 1 Damage.

[p. 12]

In response, the Marauders declare a Ranged Attack. These anti-armour weapons hit hard, but when a Unit is Engaged, Enemies are more likely to avoid taking Damage. The Roaches will be eligible for an Evade Roll in addition to an Armour Roll. Even if Katherine wanted to, she could not shoot at other enemy units, because an Engaged Unit can only target units which it is Engaged with.

Dice are rolled, many shots are fired - the Marauders hit 5 times, and the Surge Dice apply 2 surges. First, James needs to resolve an Armour Roll. He puts 2 dice directly into the Damage Pool because of Surge, and then he rolls 3 dice.

He succeeds twice, so only 1 die goes to the Damage Pool. In the Damage Pool, we now have 3 dice, and after this, James rolls for Evade - he succeeds in evading one of them.

Because 2 hits go through, it's now time to apply Damage. The damage of the Marauder's Quad K12 weapon is 1, but as the weapon has PIERCE Armoured (2), each die deals 2 Damage to the Roaches. Roaches have a Hit Points (HP) characteristic of 4. One Roach is dead and removed from the battlefield.

The first player to pass is James. He receives the First Player Marker and his Zergs will attack first in the Combat Phase!

## Round 1: Combat Phase [p. 13]

In the Combat Phase, both players can activate only Engaged Units; they must activate those units. Everyone is fighting to survive!

James, with Zerg, has the First Player Marker. He activates Roaches. Each Roach has a Rate of Attacks of 2, and hits on a 3+. Roaches successfully hit the Marauders with all 4 attacks, and the Marauders' armour value of 4+ blocks two hits. Katherine adds 2 more Damage to Marauders for a total of 3. It's still not enough to destroy one of them. At the end of that strike, James removes the Activation Marker to mark that this Roach Unit has already fought this turn.

In the first combat phase, we've only got two Units in combat, so Katherine responds with the Marauders hitting Roaches two times. Roaches are very tanky, with an Armour value of 3+. They block 1 hit, and James puts one Damage Marker next to the Unit. Katherine removes the Activation Marker from the Marauders as well to mark that this Unit has already fought.

This is the last fight of the first round.

## Round 1: Scoring Phase [p. 14]

No Unit had its supply value dropped in the first round, and controlling Mission Markers doesn't grant points in the first Round. Because the Roaches charged off of the objective marker they were on, all objectives are still unclaimed.

## Round 2: Movement Phase [p. 14]

Because neither side scored points in the last Round, both players roll off to see who will decide initiative for this Round. James rolls a 3, and Katherine rolls a 1. James decides that this time his Zergs will go first!

He activates the Queen and moves the Queen closer to the middle of the table, near the Zerglings. Next, he uses Active Ability "Spawn Creep Tumor" to put a new Creep Tumor token, using the Passive Ability "Psionic Link" to reduce the cost of "Spawn Creep Tumor" to 0.

[p. 15]

Katherine responds with a bold move of her own, closing in on the middle of the table with Marines. Just after activating Marines, she uses the Active Ability "Stim-Pack" - an iconic ability that boosts the movement and firepower of the unit at the cost of receiving some Non-Lethal Damage. She puts 2 Damage Markers next to the Marines, but it does not trigger the Remove Casualties step, despite having damage equal to the Marines' Hit Points (HP) characteristic of 2. She also needs to pay 1CP for this ability, so she chooses to use her "Barracks" Tactic Card for this.

[p. 16]

It's time for James to activate his Unit. He wants to see where the Terran player will go, so he activates Roaches and uses the Hold Action. This action allows him to activate the Unit, do nothing and end the activation. He did not plan to move because the Roaches were engaged and did not want to disengage. Their supply value was the same as the Marauders. If they disengaged, they wouldn't be able to shoot or charge in the next phase.

Katherine sees that she can dominate the middle of the table, so she brings some fire support from the reserves. StarCraft: Tabletop Miniature Game features escalation. Every player can bring something new to the table with each round. Katherine uses 1 Available Supply to bring a new unit of Marines to the party. They head towards the middle to shoot Zerglings or the Queen, and support the other Unit of Marines.

[p. 17]

James sees that his Zerglings are going to have a hard time in the middle of the board, so he decides to use some cover and help his Roaches win his right flank! He moves his Zergling and uses his "squadron" passive ability to its full extent, hiding them behind terrain pieces with a Size of 1 (statue head) and terrain with a Size of 2 - grass. This unit of Zerglings is completely hidden from the unit of Marines that just arrived, and the unit of Marines in the middle can now see only 4 Zergling models.

Katherine activates a unit of Medics, moves them closer to the injured Marines, and uses the active ability "MedPack" on them to heal the Damage caused by the stimpack. 3 Medics are in range of the Marines, so she can remove up to 3 Damage from them. The Marines only have 2 Damage on them, so the rest of the "heal" effect is lost.

[p. 18]

The Zerg player decides to bring a Unit from the Reserves. He also has 1 Available Supply and adds 12 Zerglings to the left flank. Maybe they are late to the party, but they have the potential to secure the left objective marker in the future Rounds.

Katherine decides to perform a disengage action with the Marauders and tries to hide them behind the wall. She moves the Leading Model as far as possible. In the end, she can only hide one Marauder from the Line of Sight of the Roaches. She will not be able to shoot or charge in the next Phase, because the Marauder's supply value was the same as Roaches when disengaging.

James has activated all of his Units. He decides to pass and claim the First Player Marker. Katherine also passes, and we can move to the next phase.

## Round 2: Assault Phase [p. 19]

The Zerg player has the First Player Marker. He decides to go first. He sees an opportunity to kill the Marauders with Roaches and activates them first. He knows that his Unit only sees one model, but he is fine with that. If he succeeds, the Marauders will drop in Supply to 0.

He shoots with the Roaches, using their acid-saliva ranged weapon. Each model has a Rate of Attack of 3 and hits on 3+, with each unsaved Armour Roll dealing 1 Damage. The Roaches hits 4 times. Katherine rolls for Armour, blocking the Damage on 4+, but manages to defend only 1 die! The Marauders receive 3 Damage, and adding the Damage they received earlier, this brings the total to 6 Damage - enough to kill one Marauder, which has 5 HP. There is 1 Damage leftover, but the Roaches cannot see the last Marauder, so this Damage is not applied. The Marauders lose 1 Supply Value, and James receives 1 Victory Point during the Scoring Phase.

Now it's Katherine's time for payback! She activates Marines in the middle of the battlefield. This Marine Unit is equipped with five C-14 Rifles and two SPECIALIST weapons: an AGG-12 that replaces one standard rifle, and 1 Rocket Launcher with the SIDEARM keyword, which is shoulder-mounted on a Marine. It can be used alongside a standard rifle.

Each weapon type can target different enemies, and all attacks from a weapon type must first be fully resolved before going to the next. Katherine used the Stimpack in the previous phase, so her C-14 Rifle will have PRECISION (3).

Katherine decides to shoot the Queen with AGG-12 first. This weapon has a Rate of Attack of 2 and is specialised in hunting armoured targets because of its Surge Type - Armoured. She hits twice and rolls a 2 on a D3 for Surge Value. Both hits are successful, and they go directly to the Damage Pool because of Surge. The Queen won't be able to make Armour Rolls. She receives 2 Damage.

[p. 20]

The next weapon that Katherine will use is the Rocket Launcher. She targets the Unit of Zerglings that arrived this round, standing near the Queen. The Rocket Launcher has a Rate of Attack of 4 and hits on 3+. All 4 shots find the target. The Zerglings roll for Armour on 6+ and find one success. There are 3 dice in the Damage Pool, totalling to 3 Damage, but as a reaction, James uses the Queen's Special Ability "Transfusion" to reduce the Total Damage by 2. Only 1 Zergling dies. James pays the cost of that skill with the Tactical Card "Roach Warren".

Finally, the Marines shoot with their trusty C-14 Rifles. 5 models are equipped with them, and Katherine targets the Zerglings Unit at the 2nd Mission Marker. James and Katherine discuss how many Zerglings are visible and agree that 4 are in Line of Sight. They also check the range to enemy models, and only 4 of the 5 Marines equipped with the C-14 Rifle are in Range. 4 Marines have 8 shots because of the C-14's Rate of Attack of 2. Katherine's Marines hit on 3+, and out of 8 shots, they hit with 5 and scored 1 success on the Surge Dice. Luckily, Katherine used the Stimpack on those Marines, converting 3 misses into additional hits.

One die is automatically added to the Damage Pool, and James makes an Armour Roll of 6+ for the remaining 7 dice. He managed to save 2 of them, so 6 Damage is assigned to the Zerglings. They have an HP characteristic of 1. However, before shooting, James and Katherine agreed that her Marine unit only sees 4 Zergling models. Only that many die, and the rest of the damage is discarded.

[p. 21]

Now it's James' turn to activate a Unit. He goes with the Queen, declaring a charge against the Marines in the middle. The Queen's speed characteristic is 4, but she is on creep, getting 1 more. Now the charge is successful on 2+ instead of 3+. She rolls a 2 and engages the Marines in combat. The Queen, as well as many other Zerg units, have a "Devastating Charge" ability, so she rolls 4 dice on 3+ to hit. She succeeds twice and Marines with an Armour Roll of 5+ block one hit, so Katherine puts 1 Damage Marker near the Unit.

Katherine moves the Marauders. Normally, a Unit of Marauders has a Speed Value of 4, but when there is only one model in the Unit, Katherine can use the second value of 7 and Move the last Marauder closer to the Medics.

James declares a charge with Zerglings to support the Queen with a fresh Unit of Zerglings. He needs to roll a 5+ to engage. Sadly for him, he only rolls a 4.

[p. 22]

Katherine moves the Medics closer to Mission Marker 5, towards the Queen, but not within the Unit's Engagement Range. James declares a multi-charge with a Unit of Zerglings. These were upgraded with Shredding Claws to chew through armoured Units as easily as through light Units. He declares a Charge against the Marauder and the fresh Unit of Marines. He needs to be able to engage both Units with the Leading Model to succeed with this Charge. He rolls a 6 and succeeds. Zerglings move over the grass, removing it.

Now he needs to roll for IMPACT hits. Models engaged with only one Unit must resolve impact hits against that Unit, but when engaging with multiple Units, you get to decide how to split their impact hits. James decides that 3 IMPACTs will hit the Marauder and 5 will hit the Marines.

Impact hits on Zerglings hit on 5+. He scores 1 success against the Marauder, and the Marauder fails the Armour Roll, taking 1 Damage. Next, James rolls 5 dice for Impact hits against the Marines, and he hits 4 of them. Katherine does an Armour Roll for the Marines at 5+ but fails 2 of them. She has to remove one Marine that is not engaged with the Zerglings.

[p. 23]

Next, Katherine activates the Marines, engaged in close combat with a Unit of Zerglings. They can only target the Zergling Unit they are engaged with. This Unit of Marines has no upgrades; all 5 remaining Marines will fire their C-14 Rifles. She hits 7 times. The Zerglings are engaged, so they perform an Armour Roll of 6+ and manage to succeed once. Next, the Zerglings have 6 dice in the damage pool. James now makes an Evade Roll. Zerglings are fast, especially in close combat, so they have an Evade characteristic of 4+, and he manages to succeed 3 times. At the end, he needs to deal 3 Damage to his Zergling Unit, killing 3 models that are not in base-to-base contact with the Marines. The Supply Value of these Zerglings now drops because there are only 5 left. In the Scoring Phase, Katherine will receive 1 Victory Point.

James has activated all of his Units. He passes and claims First Player Marker. Katherine has already activated everything as well and passes second.

## Round 2: Combat Phase [p. 24]

James activates the Zergling Unit Engaged with the Marauder and the Marines. All models are in base-to-base contact. James can't perform the Close Ranks. Two models are Engaged with the Marauders, two models are Engaged with the Marines and one model is Engaged with both - this individual model will target the Marauders. His surge die works against light and armoured targets, so he decides that the surge die will be used against the Marauder.

Zerglings' weapons have a RoA of 2, so 6 attacks go toward the Marauders and 4 toward the Marines. Marauders are hit 4 times, 3 of those dice go directly into the damage pool thanks to the Surge Die. The Marauder's Armour Roll is successful. They receive 3 Damage and stay on the table with 1 wound remaining!

Now for the Marines - 3 of the attacks hit, but as James has already used his Surge in this combat, he doesn't have it against the Marines. Katherine makes 3 Armour Rolls on 5+ value and fails to block all of them. 1 Marine is dead, and 1 Damage Marker is put near the Unit.

[p. 25]

Katherine activates Marines Engaged with the Queen. She can perform the Close Ranks on each model to move it closer to the target. Each Marine attacks with a Strike (RoA 1, hits on 5+). They hit the Queen 4 times, but she manages to save 2 of them with an Armour Roll of 4+.

James activates the Queen with 3 attacks on a 4+ and manages to hit once. Unfortunately, the Marines fail their Armour Roll and receive 1 Damage. The Marines already had 1 Damage in the pool, so one of them dies.

[p. 26]

Katherine activates the Marines engaged with Zerglings to attack them. She hits 3 times, but James succeeds with two Armour Rolls of 6+. Only one Zergling dies.

James has no more unactivated Units engaged with the Terran army. He passes. Katherine still has a Marauder to activate - she hits the Zerglings once, and James failed to save that hit. One Zergling dies.

This was the last attack of the second Round, and now it is time to calculate some points!

## Round 2: Scoring Phase [p. 27]

In the second Round, objectives will now give Victory Points in addition to the points that players receive for eliminating Supply. Mission Markers 1 and 2 are currently not under any player's control, but Marker 5 in the middle is a different story. James has a Queen there with a Supply Value of 1, but Katherine has 2 Units there: Marines with a Supply Value of 1 and Medics with a Supply Value of 1. She takes control of this point and is awarded 1 Victory Point.

During the Round, both sides manage to drop the Supply Value of enemy Units. Marauders lost 1 Supply, and the Zerglings lost 1 Supply as well, so both Katherine and James receive 1 Victory Point for this.

At the end of the second Round, the Terrans under the command of Katherine are winning 2 to 1 against James' Zerg. In the next Round, it will be James who decides who goes first.

That concludes two full Rounds of this epic battle. You can learn from it, or recreate this map and play the next two Rounds to see which side would come out on top.
