## Unit Abilities Summary

### Zergling
- **Claws** (Combat): E range, 2 RoA, 4+ hit, Light surge (D3), 1 damage
- **DEVASTATING CHARGE** (Passive): Immediately after successful Charge, IMPACT (1) 5+
- **METABOLIC BOOST** (Active, Assault): 1 BM - Roll D6 instead of 2D6 for Charge Distance
- **SQUADRON** (Passive): Horizontal Coherency 4"
- Upgrades: Shredding Claws (melee upgrade), Adrenal Glands (Precision 2), Burrow Ambush (deploy from anywhere)

### Raptor (Zergling Variant, Kerrigan's Swarm)
- **Claws** (Combat): E range, 2 RoA, 4+ hit, Light surge (D3), 1 damage
- **ADRENAL OVERLOAD** (Active, Assault): 1 BM - Impact Hit Rolls +1
- **DEVASTATING CHARGE** (Passive): Immediately after successful Charge, IMPACT (1) 5+
- **METABOLIC BOOST** (Active, Assault): 1 BM - Roll D6 instead of 2D6 for Charge Distance
- **SQUADRON** (Passive): Horizontal Coherency 4"
- **RAPTOR STRAIN** (Passive): Move through Size 4 or smaller IMPASSABLE TERRAIN, change elevation without access points
- Upgrades: Shredding Claws, Adrenal Glands, Burrow Ambush

### Swarmlling (Zergling Variant)
- **Claws** (Combat): E range, 2 RoA, 5+ hit, 1 damage
- **DEVASTATING CHARGE** (Passive): Immediately after successful Charge, IMPACT (1) 5+
- **METABOLIC BOOST** (Active, Assault): 1 BM - Roll D6 instead of 2D6 for Charge Distance
- **ZERGLING RECONSTITUTION** (Active, Movement): 1 BM - RESPAWN (2) or RESPAWN (3) if ON CREEP
- **SQUADRON** (Passive): Horizontal Coherency 4"
- Upgrades: Burrow Ambush

### Roach
- **Claws** (Combat): E range, 2 RoA, 4+ hit, 1 damage
- **Acid Saliva** (Assault): 8" range, 3 RoA, 3+ hit, 1 damage
- **REGENERATION** (Passive): When becomes Burrowed (Activated), HEAL (2)
- **DEVASTATING CHARGE** (Passive): Immediately after successful Charge, IMPACT (2) 4+
- **BURROW** (Active, Movement): 2 BM - Gain/lose Burrowed Status (if Unengaged)
- **GLIA RECONSTITUTION** (Active, Movement): 1 CP - BUFF Speed (1) or BUFF Speed (2) if ON CREEP
- **BURROW AMBUSH** (Passive): Deploy from anywhere via PLACE (18)
- Upgrades: Hydriodic Bile (surge change), Tunneling Claws (move while Burrowed)

### Vile (Roach Variant)
- **Vile Claws** (Combat): E range, 2 RoA, 4+ hit, 1 damage
- **Acid Saliva** (Assault): 8" range, 4 RoA, 3+ hit, 1 damage
- **DEVASTATING CHARGE** (Passive): Immediately after successful Charge, IMPACT (3) 4+
- **BURROW** (Active, Movement): 2 BM - Gain/lose Burrowed Status (if Unengaged)
- **DEBILITATING SALIVA** (Reaction, Assault): 1 BM - When declares attack on enemy within 8", apply DEBUFF Hit (1)
- **REGENERATION** (Passive): When becomes Burrowed (Activated), HEAL (2)
- Upgrades: Hydriodic Bile, Tunneling Claws, Glia Reconstitution

### Corpser (Roach Variant)
- **Claws** (Combat): E range, 2 RoA, 4+ hit, Light surge (D3+1), 1 damage
- **Acid Saliva** (Assault): 8" range, 2 RoA, 3+ hit, 1 damage
- **REGENERATION** (Passive): When becomes Burrowed (Activated), HEAL (2)
- **DEVASTATING CHARGE** (Passive): Immediately after successful Charge, IMPACT (3) 4+
- **ROACHLING INFESTATION** (Active, Movement): 2 BM, Once per Game - SUMMON (Roachling)
- **BURROW** (Active, Movement): 2 BM - Gain/lose Burrowed Status (if Unengaged)
- Upgrades: Hydriodic Bile, Tunneling Claws, Glia Reconstitution

### Roachling (Summoned Unit)
- **Claws** (Combat): E range, 2 RoA, 4+ hit, 1 damage
- **UNDERDEVELOPED CLAWS** (Passive): Cannot gain Burrowed Status

### Hydralisk
- **Scythe** (Combat): E range, 2 RoA, 4+ hit, 1 damage
- **Needle Spines** (Assault): 12" range, 3 RoA, 3+ hit, Light/Armoured surge (D3+1), 1 damage
- **LUNGE** (Reaction, Assault): 1 BM - After attack resolves, Unengaged Friendly within 10" may move DIRECTLY TOWARDS
- **SQUADRON** (Passive): Horizontal Coherency 4"
- Upgrades: Grooved Spines (Long Range 16"), Ancillary Carapace (Tough 1), Lurking (Evade while stationary)

### Queen
- **Talons** (Combat): E range, 3 RoA, 4+ hit, 1 damage
- **Talons Melee** (Combat): E range, 4 RoA, 4+ hit, 1 damage, Light surge (D3)
- **Acid Spines** (Assault): 12" range (flying), 4 RoA, 4+ hit, Light/Armoured surge (D3), 1 damage
- **DEVASTATING CHARGE** (Passive): Immediately after successful Charge, IMPACT (4) 3+
- **SPAWN CREEP TUMOR** (Active, Movement): 1 BM - Set Creep Tumor in base-to-base contact
- **TRANSFUSION** (Reaction, Movement): 1 BM - When Structure/Friendly Biological within 4" takes damage, reduce by 2
- **RESTORATION** (Reaction, Movement): 1 BM - When Friendly within 4" receives Debuff, remove all
- **PSIONIC LINK** (Passive): If 7+ models within 6", Special Ability cost reduced by 1 BM
- Upgrades: Domineering Presence (Supply +1 for markers), Creep Speed (Speed +2 on Creep)

### Kerrigan
- **Blades** (Combat): E range, 6 RoA, 4+ hit, Critical Hit (2), 2 damage
- **Energy Blast** (Assault): 8" range, 5 RoA, 3+ hit, Light/Armoured surge (D3), 1 damage
- **LEAPING STRIKE** (Active, Assault): 1 BM - PLACE (6) if Unengaged
- **DEVASTATING CHARGE** (Passive): Immediately after successful Charge, IMPACT (4) 4+
- **CRUSHING GRIP** (Active, Movement): 1 BM - Enemy within 12" counts as Activated
- **MUTATING CARAPACE** (Active, Movement): 1 BM - Enemy within 18" gains Evade against enemy's attacks with +2
- **COMMANDER** (Passive): Supply +1 for controlling/contesting/disengage
- Resources: 1 CP, 1 BM, 1 PE (costs vary by ability)

### Omega Worm
- **DETECTION** (Passive): Enemy units within 6" lose HIDDEN
- **SOURCE OF CREEP** (Passive): Friendly within 6" ON CREEP
- **STRUCTURE** (Passive): Cannot be activated, supply treated as 0, cannot control/contest
- **OMEGA NETWORK** (Passive): Each round, deploy up to Supply 2 unit via this unit's base

### Ravager
- **Claws** (Combat): E range, 2 RoA, 4+ hit, 1 damage
- **Plasma Discharge** (Assault): 12" range, 4 RoA, 3+ hit, Light surge (D3+1), 1 damage
- **DEVASTATING CHARGE** (Passive): Immediately after successful Charge, IMPACT (2) 4+
- **DEEP TUNNEL** (Active, Movement): 1 BM - Set Ravager Burrow token within 12", teleport at end of round
- **CORROSIVE BILE** (Active, Movement): 1 BM - Set Corrosive Bile tokens within 14" of models, deal HITS 5 (1)
- **SQUADRON** (Passive): Horizontal Coherency 4"
- Upgrades: Potent Bile (Armour debuff), Bloated Bile Ducts (+2" bile area), Burrow Ambush

## Faction Cards

**Zerg Swarm**
- Unique, Zerg faction
- Supply: 3 ×Core, 1 ×Elite, 1 ×Support, 1 ×Air
- Resource: 1 BM
- RAPID BURROWING (Active): Select Unengaged Ground Zerg, gain Burrowed (can be already activated)
- BROOD INSTINCT (Reaction): Before Evade Roll, +1 Modifier
- ZERG CREEP (Passive): Select exactly one Creep Card and add to army list

**Kerrigan's Swarm**
- Unique, Zerg + Kerrigan's Swarm faction
- Supply: 3 ×Core, 2 ×Elite, 1 ×Hero
- Resource: 1 BM
- MALEVOLENT MATRIARCH (Passive): On Creep Charge gains Impact +1
- LIVING GLOB OF TISSUE (Passive): Creep tokens have Stay in Play and Displacement
- CREEP REMOVAL (Passive): Creep removed when enemy ends action within 1"
- ZERG CREEP (Passive): Must select exactly one Creep Card
