# StarCraft Tabletop Miniatures Game: Changelog 09.10.2026

Source: `StarCraft-TMG-Changelog-10.2026_EN.pdf`, dated 09.10.2026.

Conventions:
- **NERF**: a cost increase (red down arrow in the source).
- **BUFF**: a cost reduction (green up arrow in the source).

---

This patch focuses on encouraging greater variety in army compositions. We’re making a range of adjustments to unit and card costs, with particular attention to Zerg, which has been underperforming relative to the other races.

---

## Terran

We underestimated the power of Stimpack. The ability provides Marines and Marauders with more mobility and offensive flexibility than expected. As a result, we are increasing their cost to better reflect their overall effectiveness.

### Marine
- NERF Mineral cost increased from 160/210 to 170/220.

### Marauder
- NERF Mineral cost increased from 150/280 to 160/290.
- NERF Veteran of Tarsonis mineral cost increased from 20/30 to 20/40.

### Medic
- NERF Mineral cost increased from 110 to 120.
- BUFF Stabilizer Medpacks mineral cost reduced from 30 to 20.

### Goliath
- BUFF Mineral cost reduced from 190 to 180.

Raynor’s Orders turned out to be more powerful than expected. Activation Marker removal allows the Terran player to easily wait out the opponent and perform their actions to create a perfect setup for themselves at the end of the Movement Phase.

### Jim Raynor
- NERF Mineral cost increased from 250 to 280.

The Academy and Barracks outshine other Terran tactical card options, being present in nearly every army. By making it slightly more expensive, we believe Terran players will give other cards a chance, such as the now cheaper Dropship.

### Tactical Cards
- NERF Barracks Vespene Gas cost increased from 25 to 30.
- BUFF Barracks (Tech Lab) Vespene Gas cost reduced from 45 to 40.
- NERF Academy Vespene Gas cost increased from 35 to 40.
- BUFF Dropship Vespene Gas cost reduced from 40 to 35.

---

## Zerg

Zerglings, Raptors, and Swarmlings all have their cost reduced to boost Zerg’s overall board presence.

### Zergling
- BUFF Mineral cost reduced from 180/220 to 150/210.

### Raptor (Zergling)
- BUFF Mineral cost reduced from 240/300 to 210/260.

### Kerrigan Swarm Raptor (Zergling)
- BUFF Mineral cost reduced from 250 to 230.

### Swarmling (Zergling)
- BUFF Mineral cost reduced from 260 to 210.

We found Glial Reconstitution to be slightly overpriced across the Roach line. Additionally, standard Roaches were more dominant than evolutions. Reducing the cost of variations should incentivise players to choose them.

### Roach
- BUFF Glial Reconstitution mineral cost reduced from 20 to 10.

### Vile (Roach)
- BUFF Mineral cost reduced from 200 to 190.
- BUFF Glial Reconstitution mineral cost reduced from 20 to 10.

### Corpser (Roach)
- BUFF Mineral cost reduced from 240 to 220.
- BUFF Glial Reconstitution mineral cost reduced from 20 to 10.

We are lowering Hydralisk upgrade costs to make customising a 3-supply unit viable.

### Hydralisk
- BUFF Ancillary Carapace mineral cost reduced from 20/40 to 20/30.
- BUFF Lurking mineral cost reduced from 10/20 to 10/10.
- BUFF Grooved Spines mineral cost reduced from 20/40 to 20/30.

### Queen
- BUFF Mineral cost reduced from 150 to 140.

Evolution Chamber and Six Pool pairs excellently with the now buffed Zerglings. We want them to be a common pick for Zergling-based armies.

### Tactical Cards
- BUFF Spawning Pool (Six Pool) Vespene Gas cost reduced from 40 to 35.
- BUFF Hydralisk Den Vespene Gas cost reduced from 35 to 30.
- BUFF Evolution Chamber Vespene Gas cost reduced from 30 to 25.

---

## Protoss

We want to improve the variety of viable Protoss army compositions. Some units have become the default choice while others struggle to justify their inclusion. By making small adjustments to the cost of individual units, we aim to encourage more diverse army builds.

### Zealot
- NERF Mineral cost increased from 160 to 170.

### Praetor Guard (Zealot)
- BUFF Mineral cost reduced from 280 to 260.

### Adept
- NERF Mineral cost increased from 150 to 160.

### Sentry
- BUFF Solid-Field Projectors mineral cost reduced from 20 to 10.
- BUFF Hallucination mineral cost reduced from 30 to 20.

### Stalker
- BUFF Mineral cost reduced from 170/270 to 160/270.

Artanis’s potential with specific tactical cards, such as Nexus, greatly exceeded our design limits. Those adjustments should remove him as an auto-pick in every Protoss army, while still leaving him as a viable option.

### Artanis
- NERF Mineral cost increased from 250 to 270.

### Tactical Cards
- NERF Nexus Vespene Gas cost increased from 35 to 40.
- BUFF Twilight Council Vespene Gas cost reduced from 45 to 40.
