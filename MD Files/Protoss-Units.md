# StarCraft TMG: Protoss Units

## Legend
- **Speed**: Unit speed / Single model speed
- **Supply**: Model count ranges with their supply costs (e.g., "1-1: 1" = 1 model costs 1 supply; "2-3: 2" = 2–3 models cost 2 supply)
- **Shield**: Unique to Protoss; represents additional damage absorption separate from HP

## Unit Abilities Summary

### Zealot
- **Psi Blades** (Combat): E range, 4 RoA, 3+ hit, Light surge (D3), 1 damage
- **CHARGE** (Active, Assault): 1 PE - Roll D6 instead of 2D6 for Charge Distance
- **DEVASTATING CHARGE** (Passive): Immediately after successful Charge, IMPACT (3) 4+
- **LEG ENHANCEMENTS** (Active, Movement): 1 PE - Move 2" (doesn't count toward action)
- **SHIELD OVERCHARGE** (Passive): First Armour Roll each round gains TOUGH (2)
- **COMBAT** (Passive): Weapon characteristics
- Upgrades: We Stand As One (Precision 2 if allied), Zealous Round (Reduce damage by 2), My Life For Aiur (Extra Impact)

### Praetor Guard (Zealot Variant)
- **Psi Blades** (Combat): E range, 4 RoA, 3+ hit, Light surge (D3), 1 damage
- **TITAN KILLERS** (Passive): Against Size 3+ targets, Damage treated as 2
- **PRECOGNITION** (Passive): Always eligible for Evade against attacks
- **CHARGE** (Active, Assault): 1 PE - Roll D6 instead of 2D6 for Charge Distance
- **DEVASTATING CHARGE** (Passive): Immediately after successful Charge, IMPACT (3) 4+
- **LEG ENHANCEMENTS** (Active, Movement): 1 PE - Move 2" (doesn't count toward action)
- **SHIELD OVERCHARGE** (Passive): First Armour Roll each round gains TOUGH (2)
- Upgrades available

### Adept
- **Strike** (Combat): E range, 1 RoA, 4+ hit, 1 damage
- **Glaive Cannon** (Assault): 8" range, 2 RoA, 3+ hit, Light surge (D3+1), ANTI-EVADE (1), 1 damage
- **PSIONIC TRANSFER** (Active, Movement): 1 PE - Set Shade token within 12", teleport models at end of round via Shade
- **PSIONIC PRESENCE** (Passive): All weapons within 4" of Shade gain PRECISION (1)
- Upgrades: Glaive Strike (melee upgrade), Resonating Glaves (RoA +1), Guidance (Anti-Evade +2)

### Nerazim Watcher (Adept Variant)
- **Strike** (Combat): E range, 1 RoA, 3+ hit, 1 damage
- **Glaive Cannon** (Assault): 8" range, 3 RoA, 3+ hit, Light surge (D3+1), ANTI-EVADE (1), PINPOINT, 1 damage
- **PSIONIC TRANSFER** (Active, Movement): 1 PE - Set Shade token within 12", teleport at end of round
- **NERAZIM FARSIGHT** (Passive): Enemy units within 6" of Shade lose Hidden
- **PATH OF SHADOWS** (Active, Movement): 1 PE - Gain Hidden until next action
- Upgrades available

### Sentry
- **Beam** (Combat): E range, 1 RoA, 3+ hit, 1 damage
- **Disruption Beam** (Assault): 8" range, 2 RoA, 2+ hit, INSTANT, 1 damage
- **GUARDIAN SHIELD** (Active, Movement): 1 PE - Friendly within 4" (no Line of Sight needed), Ranged attacks gain -1 die
- **FORCE FIELD** (Active, Movement): 1 PE - Set Force Field token within 8", blocks Size 2 or lower (Size 3+ can cross)
- **RESTORATION** (Reaction, Movement): 1 PE - Remove all Debuffs from Friendly within 4"
- Upgrades: Hallucination (Evade against Ranged), Solid-Field Projectors (Set Force Field)

### Stalker
- **Stomp** (Combat): E range, 2 RoA, 5+ hit, 1 damage
- **Particle Disruptors** (Assault): 12" range, 4 RoA, 3+ hit, Armoured surge (D3), 1 damage
- **BLINK** (Active, Movement): 1 PE - PLACE (6), models cannot end within Enemy Engagement Range
- **SQUADRON** (Passive): Horizontal Coherency 4"
- **FURY OF THE NERAZIM** (Passive, Assault): Against already-activated targets, weapons gain INSTANT
- Upgrades: Path of Shadows (Gain Hidden)

### Pylon
- **Force Field control** (Passive): Provides Warp Conduit (deploy from this base)
- **KHALAI INGENUITY** (Passive): Friendly within 4" using Psionic Energy abilities get cost reduced by 1 PE
- **STRUCTURE** (Passive): Cannot be activated, supply treated as 0, cannot control/contest markers
- **WARP CONDUIT** (Passive): Once per round, deploy Ground unit from Reserves via this Pylon

### Artanis
- **Twilight Blades Strike** (Combat): E range, 2 RoA, 2+ hit, Armoured surge (D3), 3 damage
- **Twilight Blades Sweep** (Combat): E range (6" range option), 6 RoA (2 RoA), 2+ hit, Light surge (D3+1), 1 damage
- **LIGHTNING DASH** (Reaction, Assault): 1 PE - After successful Charge, declare second Charge against different unit
- **HIERARCH'S STAND** (Reaction, Assault): 1 PE - Redirect Ranged Attack to this unit if within 8" of target
- **DEVASTATING CHARGE** (Passive): Immediately after successful Charge, IMPACT (6) 4+
- **PHASE PRISM** (Passive, Movement): When nominated to deploy from Reserves, may PLACE (0) via another Friendly
- **COMMANDER** (Passive): Supply +1 for controlling/contesting/disengage
- Resources: 1 CP, 1 BM, 1 PE

### Immortal
- **Stomp** (Combat): E range, 3 RoA, 3+ hit, 1 damage
- **Left/Right Photon Disruptor** (Assault): 12" range, 4 RoA, 3+ hit, Armoured surge (D3), CONCENTRATED FIRE (1), LONG RANGE (16), 1 damage (Sidearm variant)
- **INDOMITABLE** (Passive): While Engaged, can target/be targeted by Unengaged enemies, gain Evade
- **DEVASTATING CHARGE** (Passive): Immediately after successful Charge, IMPACT (4) 3+
- **IMPROVED BARRIER** (Reaction): 0 PE - When targeted by attack while Shielded, damage capped at 1
- **FURY UNYIELDING** (Passive, Assault): Within 3" of Mission Marker, weapons gain Crit Hit (1) vs enemy also within 3"
- **FOR THE ANCIENTS** (Passive, Assault): Beyond 8" distance, weapons gain Precision (1)
- **SHIELD OVERCHARGE** (Reaction): 1 PE - Before Armour Roll while Shielded, gain Tough (2)
- Upgrades: Left/Right Phase Disruptor (replace Photon), Force The Ancients (Precision 1)

### Zeratul
- **Master Warp Blade** (Combat): E range, 4 RoA, 3+ hit, Light/Armoured surge (D3), INSTANT, 2 damage
- **SHADOW STRIKE** (Active, Combat): 1 PE - All Engaged Enemies suffer HITS 4 (1)
- **VOID BLINK** (Active, Movement): 1 PE - PLACE (6) if Unengaged
- **DEVASTATING CHARGE** (Passive): Immediately after successful Charge, IMPACT (4) 4+
- **SENTENCED TO DEATH** (Active, Movement): 1 PE - Target Enemy unit, Zeratul's Close Combat attacks against it gain Crit Hit (2)
- **VOID PRISON** (Active, Movement): 1 PE - Set Faction Indicator within 8", enemies within 2" suffer Speed debuff (2)
- **ONE WITH THE SHADOWS** (Passive): After resolving an action, gain Hidden Status
- **PROPHETIC VISION** (Reaction): 1 PE, Repeatable - When targeted by attack, Evade treated as 4+ and cannot be modified
- Resources: 1 CP, 1 BM, 1 PE

## Faction Cards (Wave 1)

**Khalai**
- Unique, Protoss + Khalai faction
- Supply: 3 ×Core, 2 ×Elite, 1 ×Hero
- Resource: 1 PE
- BOUND BY THE KHALA (Active, Movement): At end of activation, activate another eligible unit
- DAE'UHL (Reaction): When Friendly takes damage, reduce by 2 (minimum 1)

**Daelaam**
- Unique, Protoss + Daelaam faction
- Supply: 3 ×Core, 1 ×Elite, 1 ×Support, 1 ×Air
- Resource: 1 PE
- MASS RECALL (Active, Movement, Once per Game): Set Faction Indicator, all Protoss within 6" return to Reserves
